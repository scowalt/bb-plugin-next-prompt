Suggests your next message after each agent turn, written by your OpenCode Go subscription.

## What you get

- Chips above the thread composer with a few predicted next messages, most likely first. Click one to insert its text, or × to dismiss them.
- No chips when the model sees no obvious next step.

## What it sends where

The thread title, your last three messages and the end of the agent's latest reply go to OpenCode Go. It uses the key `opencode auth login` stored on the server machine.
